# VideoMatrix v1.1.6

## What's New

- **Center-crop for mixed formats** — Videos with different resolutions are now center-cropped to match, instead of adding black bars (letterboxing/pillarboxing)
- **Default output to Downloads** — Output folder now defaults to ~/Downloads when no preference is saved, and your last-used folder is remembered across sessions

## Download

Download `VideoMatrix.dmg`, open it, and drag VideoMatrix to your Applications folder.
